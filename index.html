How I got here

  1. Phase 01 — The start

    Learning by rebuilding

    Before I had anything of my own, I rebuilt sites I liked, piece by piece, until they ran on my own machine. Most of what I know came from breaking something and then having to fix it.

  2. Phase 02 — First builds

    Pages for people around me

    Small, real jobs: a page that explains what someone does, a form that actually reaches them, a site that opens properly on a phone. Short deadlines, no place to hide.

  3. Phase 03 — 2026

    Klienera

    The biggest thing I've built so far: a business operating system for consultants, taken from a name and a blank file to a live product. Interface, database, pricing, payment rails, and the support inbox. I still run it.

    Product designFull-stackLive
  4. Phase 04 — Now

    Building for other businesses

    Pointing the same hands that built an entire product at smaller, sharper jobs: sites that have to look as serious as the business behind them, and work on the phone in a customer's hand.
